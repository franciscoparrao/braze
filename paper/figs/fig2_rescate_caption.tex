\caption{Rescuing the planner: the delivery format, not the plan,
caused the collapse. Change in pass rate relative to the same-sweep
baseline (percentage points; error bars are 95\% Newcombe/MOVER
intervals over Wilson bounds, $n{=}95$ per arm) for three deliveries
of the same planning pre-round. Rendering the plan as the
\emph{assistant's own} text (original design) collapses both executors
through empty-response degeneration; re-rendering it as \emph{user}
context rescues the capable executor (+10\,pp), while delivering it as
a typed task list re-injected compactly each round works best for the
small one (+12\,pp). The original arm was measured in the scale sweep;
the two fixed deliveries in a follow-up A/B (deltas are within-sweep;
the 3B task-list arm is a clean re-run after transient network
failures, see supplementary note).}
